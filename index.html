About

Caribbean vibes meet African electronic music. Afro house, amapiano, afrobeats, gqom, kuduro, with hip-hop and R&B roots. One goal: make people dance and connect.

Known as Aldo, Aldel was born in Paris and grew up with the sound of vinyl. He started on the hip-hop and R&B scene, where he learned to read a crowd and turn every set into an experience, before building his identity as a resident at L'Entrepôt and Le Five in Montpellier.

He won his first Red Bull Turn It Up battle at Delta Festival in Marseille, then the French final on the Warehouse stage in Nantes. He has played concert stages like the Rockstore and La Rayonne, and on Rinse France. Through his own events, TSARA'JAB and KIAFRIKA, he brings the sounds of his Malagasy roots to ever wider crowds.
